WisePage is a collaborative writing space with a built-in AI agent—where a passing thought, shared among many minds, grows page by page into something wise.

## What you can do

- **Real-time collaboration.** Your entire team writes on a single live page, with every cursor and edit synced instantly.
- **Edit with the agent.** A built-in agent that understands your workspace and references web sources to write, refine, and organize your pages.
- **One prompt, your whole workspace.** Backed by long-term memory, the agent draws on everything you've written to turn high-level intent into finished pages.
- **Type @Agent and keep writing.** Instruct the agent at any point on the page while you keep writing—it drafts in the background without breaking your stride.
- **Ask AI anywhere, in one click.** Select any passage to polish phrasing, fix grammar, translate, explain, or summarize without leaving the page.
- **Share with a few, or publish to everyone.** Invite teammates to collaborate with fine-grained view or edit access, or publish a public link for anyone to read.
- **You decide what it remembers.** Complete visibility into what the agent learns. Inspect every memory back to its source, edit or delete entries, or pause memory entirely.
